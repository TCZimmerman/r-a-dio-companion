## R/a/dio Companion

R/a/dio Companion is an unofficial third-party desktop companion for
r-a-d.io.

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official
application of r-a-d.io.

The application connects to publicly available r-a-d.io services for
audio playback and metadata. Audio content, station branding, DJ
avatars, and related material remain the property of their respective
owners.
